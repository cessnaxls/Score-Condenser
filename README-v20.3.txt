Score Condensor v20.3

- Keyboard stave assignment is now strictly pitch-based in both Literal and Intelligent modes.
- C4 (MIDI 60) and higher engrave on the treble stave.
- B3 (MIDI 59) and lower engrave on the bass stave.
- Imported MusicXML source staff numbers no longer override the keyboard split for notes.
- The split is recalculated after optional octave transcription, so folded notes appear on the stave matching their final written pitch.
